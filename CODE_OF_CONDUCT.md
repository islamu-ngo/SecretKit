# Contributor Code of Conduct

SecretKit welcomes contributors who participate respectfully and in good faith. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, community channels, and public activity by people officially representing the project.

## Working together

- Discuss ideas and implementation with evidence; critique the change rather than the person.
- Respect different backgrounds, beliefs, experiences, and accessibility needs.
- Give constructive feedback, credit sources, and take responsibility for correcting mistakes.
- Respect privacy and stated personal boundaries. Keep sensitive security reports in the private disclosure process.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, impersonation, and disclosure of private information without permission are unacceptable. Repeated disruption or attempts to evade moderation also violate this policy.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to [contact@openislamu.org](mailto:contact@openislamu.org). Describe what happened, where it occurred, and the context needed to assess it. Include only necessary personal information; do not post a report publicly. If the concern involves the usual contact, approach another verified repository maintainer where available.

Maintainers should assess reports fairly, limit disclosure to what the response requires, and give affected participants an opportunity to explain relevant context where safe. Depending on severity and recurrence, they may request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or remove access. Serious threats or sustained harassment may require immediate removal.

This is SecretKit's repository policy. Vulnerability disclosure is handled separately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
